Replace the browser-backed Codex connector with a local sign-in page and plain HTTP requests. Staff sign in on a Flockbot page served from their own Mac; the password goes only to Flocknote, and the session stays in an owner-only file. Releases now publish a Claude Desktop extension for macOS. The Codex plugins, the Windows bundle, the dedicated Chromium browser and update notices are removed.
