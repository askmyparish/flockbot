Flockbot is now an app for Mac and Windows. Sign in to your parish's Flocknote once in the app, then connect Claude Desktop, Claude Code or ChatGPT (Codex) with a click. Your sign-in stays on your computer, in the Mac Keychain or the Windows Credential Manager, and your password is never saved. The apps update themselves, and your assistant tells you when an update is ready.
